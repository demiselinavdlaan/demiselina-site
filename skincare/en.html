My skincare routine, in the right order

Morning, evening and my weekly plan, with the studies behind it.

This is my routine. My mornings are the same every day. In the evening I switch between three types of evening: tretinoin, recovery, and once a week exfoliation. The order is mostly a rule of thumb: thin to thick, active ingredients before moisturiser, and sunscreen last in the morning. What matters most is what you apply, and that you do it every day.

Every day

Morning

Six steps, in this order.

1. Cleanse

A gentle cleanser removes oil and what's left from the night.

American Academy of Dermatology: skin care order

Dermatologists' advice

2. Toner

A gentle toner. To be honest, there's no evidence a toner adds much. I let it sink in before the vitamin C goes on.

Cleveland Clinic: skin care order

A dermatologist's advice

3. Vitamin C

An antioxidant under your sunscreen. In a study, 5% vitamin C cream gave visibly smoother skin than placebo after 6 months.

Topical ascorbic acid on photoaged skin. Clinical, topographical and ultrastructural evaluation: double-blind study vs. placebo.

Humbert PG, Haftek M, Creidi P, Lapière C, Nusgens B et al.

Exp Dermatol 2003 · PMID 12823436

4. Azelaic acid

For a more even tone: it makes redness and spots less visible. In a small study, a serum with 10% azelaic acid (together with retinal) gave 18% less redness than the same formula without it. Cosmetic serums usually contain 10%. Higher strengths are prescription-only.

How I add it. From week 3 of tretinoin, every other morning at first. If it stings together with vitamin C, I alternate them by morning. Never on the same evening as tretinoin.

Study run by the manufacturer

Efficacy and safety of a novel anhydrous 0.1% retinal and 10% azelaic acid concentrate for blemish-prone skin: An eight-week randomized prospective split-face, vehicle-controlled study.

Gaudicheau C, Ahlström M, Gillbro JM, Åkerström U

Int J Cosmet Sci 2026 · PMID 42572367

5. Moisturiser

Keeps your skin hydrated and supports your skin barrier.

Role of topical emollients and moisturizers in the treatment of dry skin barrier disorders.

Lodén M

Am J Clin Dermatol 2003 · PMID 14572299

6. Sunscreen

The step with the strongest evidence, and extra important with tretinoin. In an Australian study of 903 people, daily use gave 24% less visible skin ageing after 4.5 years than occasional use. Use over half a teaspoon for face, neck and ears.

Dutch RIVM: SPF 30 or higher from UV index 3, reapply every 2 hours

Sunscreen and prevention of skin aging: a randomized trial.

Hughes MC, Williams GM, Baker P, Green AC

Ann Intern Med 2013 · PMID 23732711

Three types of evening

Evening

You rotate three kinds of evening.

Tretinoin evening

I use tretinoin, a prescription medicine, prescribed by a doctor. The key point: apply it to completely dry skin. Damp skin gives more irritation, so hyaluronic acid doesn't go on right before it on this evening. If it stings, a thin layer of moisturiser can go underneath first.

  1. Cleansing oil
  2. Gentle cleanser, pat dry
  3. Wait until your skin is fully dry
  4. Tretinoin, pea-sized amount
  5. Moisturiser

Only with a doctor. Tretinoin is prescription-only and only for use after a doctor's assessment. Not during pregnancy or when trying to conceive. In the first weeks your skin can get dry, red or flaky. That usually settles within about 4 weeks. Use SPF 30+ every day, because your skin becomes more sensitive to the sun.

Recovery evening

On evenings without tretinoin I give my skin rest and moisture. Here hyaluronic acid does go on damp skin. In a study, niacinamide (5%) reduced the look of fine lines, spots and redness after 12 weeks.

  1. Cleansing oil
  2. Gentle cleanser
  3. Toner
  4. Hyaluronic acid on damp skin
  5. Niacinamide
  6. Moisturiser

Study run by the manufacturer

Niacinamide: A B vitamin that improves aging facial skin appearance.

Bissett DL, Oblong JE, Berge CA

Dermatol Surg 2005 · PMID 16029679

Efficacy of cream-based novel formulations of hyaluronic acid of different molecular weights in anti-wrinkle treatment.

Pavicic T, Gauglitz GG, Lersch P, Schwach-Abdellaoui K, Malle B et al.

J Drugs Dermatol 2011 · PMID 22052267

Exfoliation evening

Once a week, and only once your skin is used to tretinoin. Acids make your skin more sensitive to the sun: in a study, 10% glycolic acid led to more UV damage after 4 weeks, gone within a week of stopping.

  1. Cleansing oil
  2. Gentle cleanser
  3. Acid toner (exfoliant)
  4. Hyaluronic acid on damp skin
  5. Niacinamide
  6. Moisturiser

Careful. Never on the same evening as tretinoin, and not in the first month.

Topical glycolic acid enhances photodamage by ultraviolet light.

Kaidbey K, Sutherland B, Bennett P, Wamer WG, Barton C et al.

Photodermatol Photoimmunol Photomed 2003 · PMID 12713551

Weekly plan

How to spread the evenings over your week.

How to build up

Start slowly. If your skin gets red or flaky, skip a tretinoin evening.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2: tretinoin on 2 evenings (Monday and Thursday). The rest are recovery evenings.
  2. Weeks 3 and 4: tretinoin on 3 evenings (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), and azelaic acid every other morning.
  3. From month 2: as in the week below, with one exfoliation evening on Saturday.
